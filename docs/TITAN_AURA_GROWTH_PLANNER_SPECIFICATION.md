## Required growth plan output

Every AURA Growth Planner recommendation must include:

- current business position
- growth target · target period · performance gap
- supporting evidence · assumptions
- recommended action
- expected revenue impact · expected gross-profit impact · expected net-contribution impact
- cash required · staff required · vehicle/equipment requirement · operational capacity requirement
- expected completion period · key risks · confidence level
- responsible person · due date · progress status
- Owner decision · measured outcome

### Example structure

> **Growth target:** Increase monthly revenue from R320,000 to R400,000.  
> **Gap:** R80,000 per month.  
> **Recommended actions:** Increase higher-value geyser work; improve quote acceptance; recover unbilled variations; reduce unproductive labour time.  
> **Expected result:** Revenue, cost, profit and capacity effects shown separately.  
> **Main risk:** Evidence-based capacity or cash constraints.

Do **not** present unsupported numbers as facts.

## Cash-aware growth

Must not recommend growth based on revenue alone. Consider:

available operational cash · payroll · VAT · supplier bills · vehicle commitments · marketing cost · equipment cost · expected payment timing · outstanding invoices · deposit requirements · working capital · debt exposure · job profitability · delivery capacity

- Cash constrained
- Capacity constrained
- Margin constrained
- Data incomplete
- High risk
- Owner review required
